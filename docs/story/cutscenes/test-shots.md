## 三、動畫動作描述

這部分給組員做圖生影片用，跟生圖提示詞分開。每個鏡頭只有一個主要動作。

### `c1_08` 城市（5 秒）

- 中文：鏡頭沿著大街緩慢推向總部大樓。濃煙緩緩往右飄，兩三隻烏鴉從左往右飛過，樓頂綠光微微閃爍。建築物和車輛不動。
- English: `Slow steady push-in along the avenue toward the skyscraper. Smoke columns drift slowly to the right. Two or three crows fly across from left to right. The green panel on the rooftop flickers faintly. Buildings and cars stay completely still.`

### `c2_11` 三人迎敵（4 秒）

- 中文：三人往前踏一小步站穩，風吹動頭髮和衣服。前景的殭屍慢慢逼近，紅色警示燈掃過她們的臉。鏡頭固定，輕微晃動。臉、衣服、武器保持不變。
- English: `The three women take one small step forward and brace; wind stirs their hair and coats. The foreground zombies shuffle closer. A red warning light sweeps across their faces. Fixed camera, slight shake. Faces, clothes and weapons stay the same.`（GPT 產生，再補上最後一句）

### `c2_12` A 揮棒（3 秒）

- 中文：A 從右肩後方把球棒用力揮出，打中殭屍頭部側面，殭屍向右後方倒飛出畫面，濺出少量暗紅色血。A 隨勢轉身完成揮棒動作。鏡頭固定。
- English: `A swings the bat forward hard from behind her right shoulder in one motion and hits the zombie on the side of the head. The zombie is knocked backward out of frame to the right with a small splash of dark red blood. A follows through with the swing. Fixed camera. Her hands stay on the bat the whole time.`
