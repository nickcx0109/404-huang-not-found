# 過場 2：前往總部｜Cutscene 2: To the HQ

第一章結尾 → 第二章開頭。三人找到裝甲車，衝出殭屍群，開到 MVIDIA 總部；大門鎖著，B 開始破解，殭屍從三個方向追來。

End of Chapter 1 → start of Chapter 2. The three find the armored jeep, smash through the horde and drive to the HQ. The gate is locked; B starts hacking it while zombies close in from three sides.

**基調 Mood**：前半段灰綠霧和紅色尾燈；到總部換成濕柏油的深藍灰、閃爍的綠色招牌、左右擺動的紅色警示燈。
First half: grey-green fog and red tail lights. At the HQ: wet blue-grey asphalt, a flickering green sign, red warning lights sweeping.
