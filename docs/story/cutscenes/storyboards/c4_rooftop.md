# 過場 4：頂樓，老黃被抓｜Cutscene 4: The rooftop — Lao Huang is taken

第三章結尾 → Boss 戰。三人搭電梯到頂樓和老黃會合；他正要按指紋，巨型殭屍爬上頂樓，一把抓起他吞下肚。

End of Chapter 3 → boss fight. The three ride the lift to the roof and reunite with Lao Huang. Just as he reaches for the scanner, the giant zombie climbs over the edge, grabs him and swallows him.

**基調 Mood**：頂樓強風、低雲夜空、底下城市的火光；火箭和掃描台的綠燈。Boss 出現後被陰影和暗紅吃掉。
Strong wind, low night clouds, the burning city below, green lights on the rocket and scanner. Once the giant appears, shadows and dark red take over.
