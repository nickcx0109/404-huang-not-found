# 過場 5：結局，飛向火星｜Cutscene 5: Ending — off to Mars

打倒巨型殭屍後，老黃從牠肚子裡滑出來。他按下指紋，四人搭火箭離開地球，到伊龍馬祕密發展的火星基地，和他一起建立新文明。

After the giant falls, Lao Huang slides out of its belly. He presses his palm on the scanner, the four leave Earth in the rocket, and land at the astronaut's secret Mars base to build a new civilisation together.

**基調 Mood**：打贏後的煙塵、火箭點火的白光、太空的黑；火星是暗鏽紅，這段紅色可以多用，代表新世界。
Dust after the fight, white rocket fire, the black of space. Mars is dark rust red; red may be used freely here as the colour of the new world.

**伊龍馬 The astronaut**：一律穿白色太空衣、金色鏡面面罩放下，不露臉；提示詞不寫名字。
Always in a white spacesuit with the gold mirrored visor down, face never shown; no name in prompts.
