# 過場 3：進入總部｜Cutscene 3: Inside the HQ

第二章結尾 → 第三章開頭。大門破解成功，三人退進大樓。老黃又打電話來：火箭沒油、只認他的指紋；燃料要用貨梯送上頂樓，樓梯被堵死，要找權限卡搭電梯。

End of Chapter 2 → start of Chapter 3. The gate opens and the three retreat into the building. Lao Huang calls again: the rocket has no fuel and only his fingerprint can launch it. Fuel goes up in the freight lift, the stairs are blocked, so they need a keycard for the lift.

**基調 Mood**：大樓停電幾乎全黑，只有紅色應急燈和手電筒；燃料桶用暗橘色，讓玩家一眼認出。
The building is dark: only red emergency lights and torches. Fuel barrels get a muted orange so players spot them at once.
